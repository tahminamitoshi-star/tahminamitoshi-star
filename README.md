## Research projects

**AI Adoption, SME Productivity and Employment in the European Union: Evidence and Policy Lessons for Azerbaijan**
*2nd International Conference "New Generation Economy: Challenges and Prospects", ESRI, Baku, 16 October 2026 (online). Abstract accepted.*
- Built a panel of 27 EU countries × 3 firm-size classes × 3 years (243 observations) from four Eurostat datasets
- Estimated two-way fixed effects models with country-clustered errors and a wild cluster bootstrap (only 27 clusters), plus six robustness checks including leave-one-country-out
- Found that the AI adoption gap between small and large firms widened from 22 to 38 percentage points between 2021 and 2025
- Ran a structured Scopus search with a logged two-stage screening of 474 records

**Trade Facilitation and Micro-Firm Export Entry in Bangladesh: A Critical Integrative Review and Research Agenda**
*ISRC 2026, Dhaka University Research Society, Dhaka, 28 December 2026. Abstract submitted.*
- Reviewed 60 sources (2003–2026) on customs procedures, standards, trade finance, digital capability and SME internationalisation
- Found that studies rarely measure whether micro-firms that did not export before start exporting
- Proposed three testable propositions and two model designs (discrete-time hazard, difference-in-differences) using customs, tax-registry and survey data
