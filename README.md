# Tahmina Zaman Mitoshi

Master's student in Sustainable Development at the University of Warsaw. I study how SMEs adopt AI and digital tools, and whether that adoption helps or slows their green transition. Most of my work uses official EU business statistics.

[ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-9928-7774) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ObamlG8AAAAJ&hl=en)

## Research focus

- AI adoption and digital transformation in European SMEs
- The link between digitalisation and the green transition
- Trade facilitation
- Data-driven marketing and sustainable business development

## Methods and tools

- **Python:** pandas, statsmodels
- **Statistics software:** Stata, Gretl, SPSS
- **Data:** Eurostat SDMX 3.0 API; ICT usage survey, SBS, NACE Rev. 2
- **Econometrics:** two-way fixed effects, wild cluster bootstrap
- **Other:** statistical disclosure control, reproducible research workflows, systematic literature search (Scopus)
